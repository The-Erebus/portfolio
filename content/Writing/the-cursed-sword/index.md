In a weapon store illuminated only by the sun outside and just a few candles hanging on the walls, the always smiling store owner greets a party of three that has just entered the store.

"Welcome, welcome! Make yourself at home. But don't be too comfortable, things aren't free." He says gesturing to his merchandise.

"What are you guys getting? I don't think there's anything for me here." Asks Mirah, the elf mage.

"A bow." Says Keth, the quiet rogue in the back.

"A bow? You can't use a bow, can you?" Says Terrick, the leader of the party.

"I am willing to learn. Besides, We've been too reliant on Mirah. What do you think will happen if, for some reason, we find her unable to use her spells?

"Easy, I'll protect her."

"That's not the point, but I like your enthusiasm."

"I agree..." Mirah says, slightly embarrassed.

"Well, you do what you think is best. You are our brain after all". Terrick spots a display case. It's not unlike the others, but it compels him to it.

"You know those stories where the blade chooses their wielders? I think this one chose me." He grabs the sword and gives a few swings on the air to test it. "I'm taking it."

"I don't think this is a good idea.". Mirah grabs the sword from Terrick’s hands, struggling a little with the weight. She holds it upside down to inspect the handle.

"Why?"

Keth approaches holding a hunting bow and a quiver.

"Oh, a dark blade. These things are hard to come by."

"I think it's cursed.

"Cursed?! This isn't cursed! Is this sword cursed, mister?" Terrick shouts at the store owner.

"No." The store owner responds still smiling.

"See?"

"Just look at it! There's a skull on the pommel!" Mirah brings the pommel to Terrick's face.

"That could mean anything. I bet it's a stylistic choice." Terrick grabs the sword back, and as he does, the mood shifts.

The light vanishes from the sky, and the world outside turns into nothingness. The flames on the candles dance in anticipation. The sound of footsteps travel through the air and into their frightened ears.

"Welcome, welcome!" The store owner hides behind the counter. "Make yourself at home. But don't be too comfortable, things aren't free."

"I don't think he's a client." Terrick hides the sword behind his back.

The door opens by itself revealing a slender hooded man, his head almost hitting the tall ceiling. He looks around for a long moment, taking special interest on the collection of swords. Mirah slowly moves behind Terrick. The figure takes notice of her staff and whispers something no one can hear. Pulling off his hood, he reveals his skeletal face.

"My sword... It's here..."

"It's a warlock, Terrick. Give the sword to him..." Mirah whispers.

"Not a chance. Can't you use one of your spells?"

Mirah does a gesture with her hands, but nothing happens.

"I can't"

"Why?"

"I think he's blocking my magic."

"He can do that? How's that fair? Is there anything you can do?"

"Not while he's holding the spell. I need a distraction."

"Stay behind me then." He looks around. "Where is Keth?"

"I'll kill every one here if I don't get my sword back." The warlock turns towards the two. "You!" Mirah gets closer to Terrick. "What are the two of you whispering about? You wouldn't happen to have my sword, would you?" Terrick shakes his head. The warlock laughs. "What's that behind your back then?"

Terrick looks over his shoulder and sees the frightened face of his friend. She does her best to hide it, but it’s not enough to fool someone who has been around her for so long. Holding the sword, breathing deeply, he takes a step forward, then another, until he is face to face with the tall skeleton. He raises his head, and looks deep into the warlock's eyes.

"This is a weapon store, mate. If you want a sword, you have to buy it."

“How dare you speak to me like that!” The warlock shouts, raising his right arm. Terrick begins to float. The warlock closes his fist and Terrick begins to choke, the sword still in his hand.

With teary eyes, Mirah approaches tightly holding her staff. The warlock turns his head towards her, and using the few muscles left on his face, grins maliciously. Mirah shuts her eyes.

"Keth, this is your moment." She mumbles to herself.

The moment is interrupted by the sound of an arrow passing by them. She opens her eyes and sees the warlock looking around for the origin of the sound. Aiming her staff towards the warlock, the elf whispers. A small pebble comes out of the tip of the staff, growing until it turns into a small boulder that hits the skeleton on his head. The impact is enough to break his neck. Terrick falls to the ground and prepares his attack while the skeleton regenerates.

Terrick lunges. The sword penetrates the warlock’s chest, going through without any resistance. They both fall to the ground. The skeleton, shocked, looks at Terrick and opens his jaw to speak.

"I was wrong... The sword has chosen you, not me...  From now on, the sword is yours."

The light comes back to the room, the sun illuminating the warlock’s corpse as it turn to dust. Covered with the remains of his opponent, Terrick stands up victorious.

"We did it! We actually did it! I can't believe it." Mirah runs up to Terrick and gives him a big hug. "Oh..." And accidentally covers herself with the dust.

"Sorry about that." Terrick says.

"You idiot! You almost died. Why did you not give him the sword? What if I couldn’t use my spell?" She punches him on the shoulder.

"Ouch! Because I trust you guys. That and I wanted to keep the sword."

“Looks like I will need more practise.” Keth says, coming out of the shadows. I am not hugging you. I’d rather not get my clothes dirty. But you guys do deserve my praise. That was impressive.

"I assume the curse is lifted then. Ugh, I hope I get a discount." Terrick says looking at his weapon.

The party walks to the vendor who gets up from under the counter.

"Is he gone?” The store owner asks.

"I would hope so, he turned to dust. Sorry about the mess, by the way." Terrick responds.

"And did I hear that right? He said that the sword has chosen you, didn't he?"

"Well, yes. He must’ve seen how strong I am.”

"Oh, then— hold on a second.” he pulls out a box from under the counter. Inside it, there's a sword that would go unnoticed on most stores.

"What's this?"

"A cursed sword. It chooses its wilders becoming loyal to them until they find someone better. Now it’s yours"

"But..."

"I never expected the previous wilder to come back to it. Especially after death."

"But... What about this one that I got from over there?"

"This one?" He inspects the dark blade. "This is just a ceremonial sword. It's useless for fighting. I've been trying to get rid of it for a long time, but everyone assumes that it's cursed. You can have it as well if you want. For free."

"But there's a skull on the pommel!" Terrick exclaims. Mirah looks at him incredulous.

"It's a stylistic choice."

"Of course it is."
